In this cross-platform application, users can create a list of their favourite TV shows and manage different personal statistics, such as the current watch status, when they started or finished a show, the episode they're currently at or their personal rating. To access the data, the app uses a combination of a custom backend and the public TVmaze API, where the information about the TV shows are fetched from.

<br /><br />

I built this project as a small full stack application to learn and practice some technologies that I have not used before. This was my first time using Java/Spring Boot, and while I was already familiar with Angular, I have not used it in combination with the Ionic framework before. There are of course features that could be improved or expanded further, but I also intentionally kept the project scope small, so everything, including design, backend and frontend, would be doable in a timeframe of about 2 weeks.

<br /><br />

The application is also deployed on Netlify (frontend) and Render (backend), so you can sign up and test it for yourself, however, due to the limitations of free hosting, the connection may be rather slow, and data will not be stored in the database permanently.
