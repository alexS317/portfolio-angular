I built this simple mobile app to learn Flutter, after I had done an online course on it and wanted to practise by doing a project of my own. The concept already existed before, as I had created an earlier version of the same app for a mobile coding course in the 3rd semester together with a study colleague, at that time using Kotlin. In the app, users can upload pictures of their favourite fictional characters. Whenever they open the app, they receive a random positive affirmation (which is also customisable) along with one of the images, which should help them dealing with stress and anxiety in their everyday life.

I kept the functionalities mostly the same and instead just focused on learning how to implement them with the new language. In the future I would like to try to add new features though, such as allowing users to organise the characters into folders.
