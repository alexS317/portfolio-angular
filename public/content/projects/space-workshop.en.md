For my elective course 2D Game Animation I decided to build a simple platformer game. During the first part of the course (in the 3rd semester) we learned Spine 2D, and I designed, rigged and animated the player character. The second part of the course gave an introduction to Unity’s 2D game features, where I created the game itself as my final assignment. All the assets were also drawn by myself. The course also included a small section on After Effects (which I had never used before), where the task was to create a mini-trailer for the game.
