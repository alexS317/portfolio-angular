This was the biggest project of my bachelor programme, as it was also the final one, created in the last semester which was specifically dedicated to working on one big project. The assignment was to create a serious game, so over the course of about 4 months, five other study colleagues and I created a cozy puzzle game which also teaches players about the restoration of swamp landscapes.

I was our lead for everything related to the game's graphics, and worked on concept art, 3D models and textures. Besides that, I also implemented some features in the code as well as VFX – we were a small team with little time so everyone helped with the things that were currently needed. We were really happy with the end result, and when we presented it at our university's project exhibition at the end of the semester it was very well received. Later on it was also featured in the Student Project Showcase on the official Unity Twitch channel.
