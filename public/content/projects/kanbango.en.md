In this simple project management application, users can create their own boards, customise their categories, manage tasks, invite others to their boards as well as join boards of other people upon invitation. They can also manage the roles of the board members, which determines the actions they are allowed to take.

<br /><br />

This was my first proper full stack project that I started working on. I wanted to focus on building a proper REST API and include different common features such as authentication, authorization, email invitations etc. I also wanted to become more confident at working with SQL databases and relational tables. The project is still very much a work in progress, while most of the basic features are in place in the backend by now, the frontend is still largely missing.
