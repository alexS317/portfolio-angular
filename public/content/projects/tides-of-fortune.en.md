This 3D adventure game was done for our fourth Creative Code Lab (two week intensive course at the end of the semester) and was done as a group project with three of my study colleagues. I was responsible for creating the main character with all of their features (concept, modelling, texturing, rigging, animating), as well as some other graphical assets, both 2D and 3D, like the treasure chest or the inventory UI. I also implemented some features in the code, like the item collection. The project was intense, but also very fun to work on, and even though none of us in the team had worked with Unity prior to the semester, we were very happy with what we could achieve in this short amount of time.
